working together

I work with companies when their products are a good fit for the channel.

The usual format is a 30–60 second sponsored integration in a regular Hacktic video. I write and present the segment myself.

Developer tools, hardware, game-development products, hosting and creative software are generally the strongest fit.

I'm also open to dedicated videos when the product itself supports a strong video idea.
